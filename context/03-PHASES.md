# Development Phases

## Phase 1 — Launch Ready (Core Platform)

**Goal**: Companies can register, upload products (including CSV), accept payments, manage orders, and go live.

### Must Include
- Multi-tenancy foundation
- Company registration & onboarding
- Authentication (Clerk) + Staff roles
- Product management + Variants
- **Bulk CSV/Excel upload**
- Inventory (basic)
- Orders + Checkout
- Paystack payments
- Basic Discounts/Coupons
- Multi-currency (basic)
- Email + SMS notifications
- Basic Analytics
- Basic Delivery (zone-based rates)
- SaaS Billing (subscription plans)
- Clean Admin Dashboard
- Usable Customer Storefront

### Phase 1 Exit Criteria
- New company can be live in under 20 minutes
- Bulk upload works reliably
- Real orders can be placed and paid
- Platform can charge monthly subscription

---

## Phase 2 — Strength & Scale

**Goal**: Make the platform more powerful and reduce manual work.

### Focus Areas
- Advanced Delivery (weight-based, logistics integrations)
- Full Custom Domain support
- Better SMS & Email templates
- Deeper Analytics
- Improved Inventory & low-stock alerts
- Better Admin UX (bulk actions, faster tables)
- Abandoned cart notifications
- Performance improvements

---

## Out of Scope (For Now)
- Mobile App
- Multi-language
- Crypto payments
- Complex marketplace features
- AI features
