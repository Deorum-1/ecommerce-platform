# Features Priority

## Must Have (Phase 1)
- Company registration & onboarding
- Bulk product upload (CSV / Excel)
- Product variants (size, color, etc.)
- Inventory management
- Order management
- Discount / Coupon system
- Multi-currency support
- Analytics dashboard (basic)
- Staff roles & permissions
- Email notifications
- SMS notifications
- Basic Delivery (zone-based)
- Custom domains (preparation)
- Subscription billing for companies

## Nice to Have
- Customer accounts
- Advanced analytics
- Abandoned cart recovery

## Not Needed (for now)
- Multi-language
- Mobile app
- Crypto payments
