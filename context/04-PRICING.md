# Pricing Strategy (Summary)

**Currency**: Naira (₦)  
**Model**: Monthly subscription  
**Platform fee on sales**: 0% at launch

## Proposed Plans

| Plan        | Monthly Price | Yearly (approx) | Target                     |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|----------------------------|
| Starter     | ₦9,500        | ₦95,000         | Small shops / testing      |
| Growth      | ₦22,000       | ₦220,000        | Serious single-store       |
| Business    | ₦39,000       | ₦390,000        | Growing teams              |
| Enterprise  | Custom        | Custom          | Larger / multi-brand       |

## Key Rules
- 14-day free trial on all plans
- Yearly billing gets 15–20% discount
- Higher plans unlock more products, staff accounts, SMS credits, and custom domains
- No percentage taken from store sales

Full details: `docs/PRICING_STRATEGY.md`
