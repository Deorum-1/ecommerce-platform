# Project Overview

**Name**: Multi-Tenant E-Commerce SaaS Platform  
**Market**: Nigeria  
**Model**: Public SaaS – shopping companies register and pay monthly subscription  
**Scale (Year 1)**: ~5 companies  
**Builder**: Solo (with AI guidance)  
**Design Style**: Simple & functional (inspired by Jumia)

## One-line Promise
The easiest multi-store e-commerce platform for Nigerian businesses — especially those who already manage products in Excel.

## Core Value Proposition
- Extremely fast company onboarding
- Excellent bulk CSV/Excel product upload
- Reliable Paystack payments
- SMS-first notifications
- Clean, mobile-friendly admin dashboard
- Simple and honest Naira pricing

## Business Model
- Companies (tenants) register and get their own store + admin
- They pay a monthly subscription
- Platform takes **0%** of their sales (only subscription revenue)
- Primary payment gateway for stores: Paystack
